# Palmer Penguins

## Overview

Morphological measurements of penguins collected at Palmer Station, Antarctica, by Dr. Kristen Gorman and the Palmer LTER program. The table holds 344 penguin observations.

## Schema

Three species appear: Adelie, Chinstrap and Gentoo. Sampling covered three islands named Biscoe, Dream and Torgersen. Field seasons ran from 2007 through 2009. Body mass ranges from 2,700 to 6,300 grams. Average bill length is 43.9 mm.

## Data quality

Measurements are largely complete, with two caveats: the four bill and flipper measurements each have a couple of gaps, and the `sex` column is missing for 11 birds.

## Caveats

Units are millimetres for lengths and grams for mass; `year` is the field season, not a hatch year.
