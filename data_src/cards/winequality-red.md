# Red Wine Quality

## Overview

Physicochemical measurements paired with sensory quality scores for Portuguese Vinho Verde red wines, assembled for quality-prediction research by Cortez and colleagues. The dataset covers 1,599 red wine samples.

## Schema

Eleven numeric inputs describe each wine and one integer output grades it. Quality scores range from 3 to 8. pH values fall between 2.74 and 4.01. Alcohol content spans 8.4 to 14.9 percent.

## Data quality

There are no missing values anywhere in the table. The mean quality score is 5.6.

## Caveats

Quality is the median of at least three blind sensory assessments; classes are imbalanced toward scores 5 and 6.
